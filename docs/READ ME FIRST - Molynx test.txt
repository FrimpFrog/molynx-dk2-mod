MOLYNX TEST - Dungeon Keeper 2 (Flame)
======================================

Molynx is a brand-new 47th creature: a big red rock golem with horns and a mace.
He is only on two new test maps, MolynxTest1 and MolynxTest2. Your other maps are not changed.

You need: Dungeon Keeper 2 with Flame already installed (you start the game with DKII-DX.exe).


INSTALL (about 2 minutes)
-------------------------

1. BACK UP 3 FILES. In your Dungeon Keeper 2 folder, copy these somewhere safe:
      PATCH.dll
      flame\DKII.dll
      flame\Flame.dll

2. COPY. Open the folder "Copy into your Dungeon Keeper 2 folder" in this zip.
   Copy everything inside it (PATCH.dll and the folders Data and flame) into your
   Dungeon Keeper 2 folder. When Windows asks, choose "Replace the files".

   (The PATCH.dll and flame files are a Flame build with a small fix that lets the
   game have more than 46 creatures. Without it the game freezes when the map starts.)

3. TURN MOLYNX ON. Open  flame\config.toml  with Notepad.
   Find the line that starts with  alt-resources  and put  Molynx;  right after the
   first quote mark. For example:

      alt-resources = "Quuz_AudioFix;LevelFixes;Quuz_UniqueBatTex"

   becomes

      alt-resources = "Molynx;Quuz_AudioFix;LevelFixes;Quuz_UniqueBatTex"

   Molynx must be FIRST. Save the file.
   (Or in the Flame start window: double-click Molynx so it is Enabled, then drag it to the top.)


PLAY
----

Start the game, go to Skirmish and pick a map:

  MolynxTest1 - Molynx stands next to your Dungeon Heart. Flat open map, lots of gold,
                every room and spell, and no enemies to bother you.
  MolynxTest2 - the same, but more Molynx can also come through your Portal.
                Build a 5 x 5 Hatchery and a 10 x 10 Combat Pit to attract him.

If you cannot see the maps in the Skirmish list: in flame\config.toml change
  Level = ""   to   Level = "MolynxTest1"
save, and start the game. Put it back to  Level = ""  afterwards.


WHAT TO TRY
-----------

Build him a Lair, Hatchery, Training Room, Workshop, Temple and Torture Chamber, then watch him:
walking, standing about, fighting/training, eating, sleeping, praying, working, being picked up,
dropped and slapped, his creature panel and pictures, his sounds, and his special attack
Ground Slam (he smashes his mace into the floor).

Tell us anything that looks wrong, ugly, too strong, too weak, or that you would change.


WHAT TO SEND BACK
-----------------

- What you liked and what should be better.
- Screenshots or a short video if you can.
- If the game crashes or freezes: what you were doing at that moment, and these files
  (copy them straight after the game closes, the next start overwrites them):
      flame\latest.log
      flame\molynx.log
      the newest file in flame\CrashInfo  (if there is one)
      the newest file in flame\StackInfo  (if there is one)


REMOVE IT AGAIN
---------------

1. Take  Molynx;  out of the alt-resources line in flame\config.toml.
2. Delete the folder  flame\resources\Molynx
3. Delete the 14 files  Data\editor\maps\MolynxTest1*  and  MolynxTest2*
4. Put back your 3 backed-up files (PATCH.dll, flame\DKII.dll, flame\Flame.dll).
