# Classic Reaper credits

- **Face, torso, back and legs art: Quuz.** The Classic Reaper wears Quuz's DK1-style reaper face
  (`ReaperFaceSmirk`) and his painted shoulder plates, chest and back chains, chain belt and loin flap
  (`ReaperTorso`, `ReaperBack`, `ReaperLegs`) from his "HD textures" pack for Flame. The face is used as he
  made it (only brought up to 128 x 128 and its red matched to the body); his shoulder plates are turned
  shiny silver, a patch of his plate art covers the round shoulder pads, and the skin is evened out. The
  design is 100% his.
- This repository does not hold copies of Quuz's textures. `scripts/reaper_skin.py` reads them from the
  player's own copy of the pack (`flame/resources/Quuz_HD/Data/EngineTextures`, or the folder the
  `QUUZ_HD` setting names) when the skins are built.
- **Model, base textures, horns and scythe:** Bullfrog Productions (Dungeon Keeper 2's Horned Reaper, "Horny"),
  read from the player's own game.
- **DK1 reference sprites:** Bullfrog Productions (Dungeon Keeper).
